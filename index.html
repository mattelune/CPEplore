01 / Introduction

A new era of learning begins with AI.

Artificial Intelligence is increasingly becoming part of modern education. From generating explanations to assisting with programming, AI-powered tools are changing how students approach academic tasks.

For first-year Computer Engineering students, these technologies present both opportunities and challenges. Understanding their influence is important as students begin developing the technical knowledge and problem-solving skills required in the field.

This research explores the relationship between AI use and the learning experiences of first-year CpE students.
